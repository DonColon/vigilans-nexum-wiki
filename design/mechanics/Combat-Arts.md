# Combat Arts

The active weapon techniques of Vigilans Nexum: what a combat art is, what it costs, how a non-mage builds the MP to pay for it, and why it is the non-mages' door into the element system. This document holds the rules, and its values are in its own [Balancing](#balancing) section. Which arts exist – personal, class, weapon and general – is in the [catalog → Combat Arts](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md).

## Overview

Combat arts are **active techniques for the Player Phase**. The player chooses them on purpose, as a unit's action, and pays for them in MP – unlike [abilities](Abilities.md), which trigger on their own and cost nothing but a slot. Every art carries an **element** – the improvised arts of the Ch 01 prologue excepted – and for a non-mage an art is the only ordinary way to put an element on an enemy or set one off. A non-mage **builds** the MP for its arts with ordinary attacks, so an art is something a unit fights toward rather than something it opens with.

The purpose in one sentence: **the player decides when the MP a unit has built up is worth spending – on which art, on which target, and whether this unit sets the element or sets off the one already there.**

|           | Combat Arts |
| --------- | ----------- |
| Phase     | Player Phase |
| Trigger   | Chosen by the player as the unit's action |
| Resource  | MP |
| Element   | Every art carries one, except the prologue arts of Ch 01 (*Core Rules → 4*, *Open Decisions → 1*) |

## Core Rules

### 1 – What an art is

An art is an **attack or effect the unit chooses as its action on its own turn**. It does what its catalog row says, on top of or instead of an ordinary attack, and it carries the element named in its row. Arts are not used on the Enemy Phase: a counter-attack is always an ordinary attack.

### 2 – Arts and the element system

For a **non-mage**, arts are the only way to apply an element and to trigger a reaction. The one written exception is Dardan's [*Wavelength*](Nexus.md#8--wavelength): while it runs, his **ordinary** attacks apply a borrowed element – and never trigger one ([Magic System → How elements are applied](Magic-System.md#3--how-elements-are-applied)). The exception is named here so that neither document can be read alone and be wrong.

| Action | Result |
| ------ | ------ |
| Art on an enemy without an element | The art's element is applied |
| Art on an enemy with an element | The elemental reaction for the pair is triggered |
| Ordinary attack (non-mage) on an enemy with an element | Damage – the element **stays** on the enemy |

A non-mage therefore has to invest MP to take part in the element system at all. Arts are not only damage options; they are the non-mages' reaction triggers. The reactions themselves, and what they leave behind, are the [Magic System](Magic-System.md#4--how-reactions-are-triggered)'s.

**What an art does while Wavelength runs.** Wavelength changes Dardan's ordinary attacks only; an art does what the art says, including applying its own element and triggering a reaction as any non-mage's art does ([The Nexus → Interaction](Nexus.md#interaction-with-other-mechanics)). The one edge – an art that carries **no element of its own** – is *Open Decisions → 1*.

### 3 – MP: building and spending

A **non-mage** spends no MP on ordinary attacks. Every ordinary attack **builds** MP, more with higher Mag ([Balancing](#mp-gain-per-ordinary-attack)), and arts spend it. Mag therefore matters to a non-mage as well: a higher Mag builds MP faster and allows arts more often. The regime is the [Magic System](Magic-System.md#non-mages--active)'s; this document holds its values because the MP it builds exists only to be spent on arts.

A **mage** pays for an art out of the same pool that pays for its tome attacks and regenerates by the round. A **Citizen** follows the regime of its equipped weapon ([Growth Modifiers → Core Rules → 5](Growth-Modifiers.md#5--a-citizens-mp-mode-follows-the-equipped-weapon)).

### 4 – Learning and keeping

Arts come from four sources, the four sections of the [catalog](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md):

- **Class arts** are taught by the class, at the tiers the catalog names, and are never obtainable any other way.
- **Weapon arts** are tied to a weapon type: any unit that holds the type can learn them, from the tier the catalog names.
- **General arts** are taught to every class at the tier where arts open. Their element is the primary element of the wielder's weapon type.
- **Personal arts** belong to one character and are set on that character's sheet.
- **Prologue arts** exist in Ch 01 only (decided by Dardan, 2026-09-29). In Ch 01 the Vigilant Knights are children, mechanically Citizens at level 1, and their arts are **improvised actions the chapter's prose shows them doing** – each held by the child the text gives it to. They carry no element, are held from the start of the fight that shows them to the end of the chapter, and then disappear; no unit ever has them again. They are listed in the [catalog → Prologue Combat Arts](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md#prologue-combat-arts-ch-01-only).

There are **no art scrolls**, unlike abilities. Every learned art is kept **permanently**, through every promotion – the prologue arts being the one exception, because nothing carries over from Ch 01 ([Progression System](../Progression-System.md#part-01-path-of-liberation-chapters-1-8)). Because promotions cannot be undone, the arts a unit will ever hold are decided at the four forks of its class tree.

### 5 – Both sides

Enemies use arts under the same rules: an enemy of a class holds that class's arts and pays for them in MP. [Earthbound](Nexus.md#5--earthbound) seals an enemy's arts together with its abilities, magic and auras, which presumes exactly that.

## Strategic Depth

Arts are the link between physical units and the element system:

- **Access to elements.** Without arts, non-mages cannot apply elements and cannot trigger reactions – they are shut out of the strongest part of the damage system.
- **The MP rhythm.** MP is built by ordinary attacks, so a rhythm forms: attack, build, spend at the right moment. Spending too early wastes the build-up; a unit that arrives at the boss with an empty pool arrives without its trigger.
- **Setter or trigger.** The same art sets an element on a clean enemy and sets off a reaction on a marked one. Which unit strikes first decides which reaction happens, and whether it happens at all.
- **Chain Attack preparation.** Inside a [Chain Attack](Chain-Attack.md), all three units may use their arts, and the order of the arts decides the reactions. Who arrives with enough MP is decided turns earlier.
- **Class identity.** Because class arts are class-bound and promotions are permanent, each fork fixes which arts a unit will ever hold, and with them which elements it can bring to a reaction.

## Design Pillars

*Proposed by Rulewright, pending Dardan's review (2026-09-29).*

| Pillar | Question | Answer |
| ------ | -------- | ------ |
| **Bonds** | Does it strengthen the connections between units? | **Partly.** An art is one unit's action, but its point is usually another unit's element: the art that triggers a reaction finishes what someone else started, and *Bonded Strike* borrows an adjacent ally's element outright. The system reads no affinity rank, so the connection is tactical rather than a bond in the Affinity sense |
| **Depth** | Easy to learn, hard to master? | **Yes.** "Spend MP for a special attack" is understood at once. Mastery is the MP rhythm across a map, the choice between setting and triggering, and holding MP back for a Chain Attack |
| **Weight** | Do the decisions have long-term consequences? | **Yes, at the forks; within a battle, for its length.** Class arts are part of an irreversible promotion, so the fork decides which arts a unit will ever have. Within a map, MP spent now is MP missing later |
| **Integration** | Does the mechanic tell a story? | **Yes.** Each weapon's element follows the physics of its strike and the school that wields it ([catalog → Weapon Combat Arts](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md#weapon-combat-arts)), and Ch 19 names arts as a Thysian discipline. Where the system is introduced, the arts are what the chapter's prose shows the children doing – Dardan's blow at the legs of the older boy, Maksimo's sand in the soldier's eyes – so the first art the player uses is a line he has just read, and it disappears with the childhood it belonged to |
| **Fairness** | Is the challenge respectful of the player's time? | **Yes.** The cost, the MP left, the element and the reaction are in the forecast before the art is chosen; the MP a unit will build is shown on its bar. Nothing is rolled that the forecast does not show |

**Anti-pillar check:** **No grinding:** there are no art scrolls, and arts come from class, tier and weapon type, not from repetition. **No power without a price:** every art costs MP built by earlier attacks and takes the unit's action. **Nothing that works only with over-trained units:** a basic art costs two or three attacks' worth of MP at any level. **Nothing generic:** combat arts are Fire Emblem's (*Three Houses*); what is this game's own is that every art carries an element and that arts are the physical units' only door into the reactions.

**Is there a combination that trivialises a fight?** The hybrids that set and trigger a reaction alone (two weapon types whose elements react, or a two-element class art) are the strongest case. They pay for it by capping one rank lower in their second type and, for the two-element class arts, with a higher MP cost ([catalog](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md)).

## Introduction

**First chapter:** **Ch 01**, *Every End…*, in **fight 2** – the fight against the older children on the market square, objective *Besiege den Anführer*. Decided by Dardan (2026-09-29). The chapter's tutorial box for this fight already lists *Kampfkünste*, beside *Waffenvorteile* and *Umgebungsvorteile* ([Ch 01](../../story/chapters/Part-01-Path-Of-Liberation/Chapter-01-Every-End.md)).

**How it is introduced:** through an **improvised action the prose already shows** (decided by Dardan, 2026-09-29). The orphans fight with sticks, wooden boards and old pans against children who are older and stronger. In the text, Dardan strikes the blond leader's legs and throws him off balance, and Hasan uses the opening to hit him – that is *Leg Sweep* ([catalog](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md#prologue-combat-arts-ch-01-only)): a unit that has attacked has built MP, and the art is how Dardan's blow sets up Hasan's. The fight's objective is the leader, so the art points at the very target the map asks for. How the map places it is Level 01's (`levelcraft`). In fight 3 Maksimo's *Sand in the Eyes* follows, and both arts can be used inside the [chain attack](Chain-Attack.md#introduction). Magic does not exist yet, so the arts carry no element and teach no reactions; that lesson comes in Ch 05 ([Magic System → Introduction](Magic-System.md#introduction)).

**The prologue arts do not last.** Ch 01 is a self-contained prologue, and nothing carries over into Ch 02 (*Core Rules → 4*). What the player keeps is the lesson: MP is built by attacking and spent on a technique.

**What the player must already know:** movement, attacking and items – fight 1 of the same chapter.

**Level index.** Ch 01 carries *Combat Arts* in the *New Mechanics* column of the [level index](../levels/README.md#part-01-path-of-liberation) and in the [Progression System](../Progression-System.md#part-01-path-of-liberation-chapters-1-8).

## Balancing

This section holds the values that belong to combat arts alone. It was created on 2026-09-29, when the MP gain per attack moved here out of the core rules; the same table stood in the [Magic System](Magic-System.md#non-mages--active) and was removed there, so it now exists once. Each art's MP cost is an entry and lives in the [catalog](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md), together with the catalog's rule of thumb for pricing arts against the MP a unit builds.

### MP gain per ordinary attack

A non-mage's MP gain per ordinary attack, by Mag.

| Mag | MP per ordinary attack |
| --- | ---------------------- |
| 0–5 | +1 |
| 6–12 | +2 |
| 13+ | +3 |

### Prologue values

*Proposal (2026-09-29).* The values of the Ch 01 prologue arts ([catalog](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md#prologue-combat-arts-ch-01-only)). They exist in Ch 01 only.

| Parameter | Value | Why |
| --------- | ----- | --- |
| MP cost of *Leg Sweep* and *Sand in the Eyes* | 2 | A Citizen's Mag is about 3 ([Base Stats at Level 1](../Balancing-Guide.md#base-stats-at-level-1-citizen-class)), which builds 1 MP per ordinary attack in the table above. Two attacks, then the art: the low end of the catalog's "a basic art after two or three attacks", so the lesson arrives within one short fight |
| Hit lost by the target of *Sand in the Eyes* | −10 | The same size as the [Battle Staff Guard](../Balancing-Guide.md#battle-staff-guard): the existing value for a small, situational Hit penalty, and below the triangle swing of 15 that fight 2 has just taught, so the sand never outweighs the right weapon |

### What each value tunes

| Parameter | What it tunes |
| --------- | ------------- |
| MP gain per ordinary attack | How many attacks an art costs – the tempo of the MP rhythm – and how much Mag is worth to a non-mage |
| Each art's MP cost (catalog) | Where on that rhythm the art sits: a basic art after two or three attacks, a class signature after four or five |
| Prologue values | How soon the Ch 01 arts arrive and how much the sand protects the children in fight 3 |

**Rules, not tuning values**, and therefore in the rules above:

- Non-mages build MP only with ordinary attacks and spend it only on arts.
- An art is the unit's action on its own turn, never a counter.
- Every art carries an element, except the prologue arts; a non-mage applies and triggers only through arts, with *Wavelength* as the one exception.
- There are no art scrolls, and learned arts are permanent – except the prologue arts, which exist in Ch 01 only.
- The system starts in Ch 01; its elements do nothing before Ch 05.

## UI & Display

- **The art menu** lists each learned art with its MP cost and element; an art the unit cannot pay for, or whose weapon is not equipped, is greyed out with the reason.
- **The battle forecast** shows the MP cost, the MP left, the element and – if the target carries an element – the reaction by name.
- **The MP bar** of a non-mage shows how much its next ordinary attack will build.

## Open Decisions

Everything below is written into the rules above in its **cautious form**. Each is Dardan's to confirm or change.

**Decided by Dardan and therefore not listed:** arts cost MP, are class-bound with no scrolls and are kept through promotion; non-mages build MP by attacking; *Wavelength* is the one exception to "only through an art" (2026-09-22); the system is introduced in Ch 01, fight 2 (2026-09-29); **the Ch 01 arts are improvised actions taken from the prose, held by the children the text shows doing them, Citizens at level 1, and they exist in Ch 01 only – nothing carries over from the prologue** (2026-09-29, closing former item 2 as it stood).

1. **An art with no element of its own.** Every art outside the prologue carries an element (the general arts take the weapon's primary element). The two prologue arts carry none, so elementless arts now exist – but only in Ch 01, where *Wavelength* does not. [The Nexus → Open decisions → 29](Nexus.md#open-decisions) asks what an elementless art does while *Wavelength* runs – carry the borrowed element, and so trigger, or not. **Not resolved here.** It still cannot arise in play; the moment an elementless art is added outside the prologue, Nexus 29 has to be answered first.
2. **What remains of the Ch 01 art question.** The arts, their holders and their scope are decided. Open are only the proposals: the names, effects and values in the [catalog](../catalog/Combat-Arts.md#prologue-combat-arts-ch-01-only) and [Prologue values](#prologue-values), and whether the two prologue arts count as the *certain combat arts* that wear down a shield (proposed yes, [Chain Attack → Open Decisions → 3](Chain-Attack.md#open-decisions)).
3. **Enemies and arts.** Written as symmetric (*Core Rules → 5*), because Earthbound's seal presumes enemies hold arts. Which enemies hold which arts is the class catalog's and the level documents'.
4. **Arts learned by level-up.** The old document named "occasionally new arts within the class" on level-up, and the [Progression System](../Progression-System.md#-skill--ability-unlocks) says arts are "learned at specific class levels". No catalog entry names a level. Conservative: arts come with class entry and tier as the catalog lists them; level-gated arts would need a level column in the catalog.
5. **Weapon Uses.** Whether an art spends a Use of the weapon, as the ordinary attack it replaces would, is not written. Conservative reading: one Use, like the attack it is. The alternative – Fire Emblem's extra durability cost for arts – would add a second price.
6. **"Thysian Combat Arts" in Ch 19.** The [Progression System](../Progression-System.md#part-03-the-champions-road-chapters-17-24) still lists it as the mechanic introduced in Ch 19. Now that combat arts are introduced in Ch 01, what Ch 19 adds is undefined – a Thysian school of arts, the Intermediate weapon arts, or nothing new. Left as it stands.
