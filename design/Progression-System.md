# Progression System

How characters, classes, and abilities develop across all 64 chapters of Vigilans Nexum.

---

## 🎯 Overall Progression Arc

**Total Game Length:** 64 Chapters across 8 Parts
**Chapters per Unit:** 52 progression chapters – Parts 01–04 (32) + one strand of Part 05/06 (8) + Part 07 (8) + Tower (4). The epilogue (Ch 53–56) grants no XP.
**Level Range:** 1 → 60, cap reached at the Dajjal (Ch 52)
**Pace:** ~1.13 levels per progression chapter
**Class Tiers:** Citizen → Base → Intermediate → Advanced → Master *or* Unique

**Design intent:** The eight Vigilant Knights are the only units that start at Level 1 in the Citizen class. Everyone else joins at their established experience level. Over 59 level-ups the Bellum orphans overtake the veterans who joined ahead of them – chosen family outgrowing inherited status, expressed as a stat sheet rather than a line of dialogue.

### Part 05 / Part 06: The Parallel Strands (Chapters 33-40)

**Level Range:** 37 → 45 – **identical in both strands**
**Enemy Levels:** 39 → 47 – **must be identical in both strands**
**Focus:** Split roster, two independent 8-chapter campaigns

| Strand | Chapters | New Units |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Part 05 – Divided We Face Our Sins (Dardan, Adolla) | 33A–40A | Jessy Harvel, Aldric Halvorn, Edric Thalven, Maluna Eloise |
| Part 06 – Divided We Find Our Virtue (Hasan, Vaestrall) | 33B–40B | Aylin Vaestrall, Rijona Alior, Corvan Gault, Maike Collis |

**Why no special XP rule is needed:** Both strands are exactly eight chapters long and share one level band. As long as enemy levels match, both groups arrive in Part 07 at the same strength. The split resolves itself.

**What is needed instead – catch-up XP:** With 30+ recruited units and 14–16 deployment slots, the spread *within* a group is larger than the spread *between* the groups. Units below their group's average level gain bonus XP so the bench does not fall out of the game.

**Milestone:** Both groups reach Lv 45 at the end of their strand and reunite in Ch 41.

**Level Range:** 54 → 60 (cap)
**Enemy Levels:** 56 → 62, all promoted
**Pace:** ~1.5 levels per chapter – justified by promoted enemies (+5 XP) and boss kills (×3 XP)

**Ch 52 is the last deployment decision of the game** – who fights the Dajjal is chosen here.

#### The Epilogue (Chapters 53-56) – no progression

**Level:** 60, static. No XP, no promotions, no seals. Every unit's class is final long before this point.

The journey through Adolla, Thysia and Vaestrall, gathering people until all roads lead to Bellum. The Nexus bond is finished as a combat tool at Ch 52; here it pays off as a relationship. **Support ranks reach their maximum in these four chapters** – that is their mechanical purpose.

> **Permadeath consideration:** `Pacing-Audit.md` marks Ch 52 as the intended place for a lasting loss. Four epilogue chapters have to carry that loss without breaking their fixed text – whoever falls at the Dajjal must stay gone through the festival.

## 🏆 Class Progression System

### Class Tiers & Requirements

```
Citizen (Lv 1)
    ↓ [End of Chapter 06 – story event, no level requirement]
Base Classes (Swordsman, Archer, Pyromancer, ...)
    ↓ [Level 15 + Seal]
Intermediate Classes (Myrmidon, Sniper, Pyro Sage, ...)
    ↓ [Level 30 + Seal]
Advanced Classes (Sword Master, Assassin, Elementalist, ...)
    ↓ [Level 45 + Seal]
Master Classes (Sword Saint, Nightveil, Arcanist, ...)
    │
    └─ [Level 45 + Story unlock] → Unique Classes (Aetherblade, Ashborn, ...)
                                    Dardan and Hasan only
```

**Unique classes are an alternative to Master, not a tier above it.** Both promote from Advanced, both are terminal, and both open at Lv 45. Per [catalog/Unit-Classes.md](catalog/Unit-Classes.md) the Unique classes are restricted to Dardan and Hasan and unlock through story events rather than a purchasable seal.

That makes **four promotions** across the campaign, not five.

### Promotion Requirements

**Level and a seal – nothing else.** There are no stat requirements. A unit that reaches the level can always take the promotion, and no unit can ever be locked out of its branch by unlucky growth rolls. The first step is not even level-gated: the base class is chosen in a story event, so no unit can arrive in Part 02 still a Citizen.

| Tier | Requirement | Seal Cost |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Base | End of Chapter 06 (story event) | Free |
| Intermediate | Level 15 | 500 Gold |
| Advanced | Level 30 | 2,000 Gold |
| Master | Level 45 | 5,000 Gold |
| Unique | Level 45 + story unlock (Dardan/Hasan) | Cannot buy |

Seals are **promotion items, not reclassing items** – each one is consumed to move a unit one tier up its own branch. They cannot move a unit sideways into a different class line.

**Why no stat gates:** With permanent promotions, a stat requirement would be a second irreversible failure mode – a unit could miss its gate through bad RNG and stay stuck a tier below the rest of the army for the remaining campaign, with no way to correct it. Level alone is the honest gate: it measures what the player invested, not what the dice granted.

### Class Change Rules

- **Promotion is permanent.** There is no reclassing. A unit moves up its branch and never sideways or back – once chosen, a class is that unit's identity for the rest of the campaign.
- **Every promotion is a fork.** The class tree in [catalog/Unit-Classes.md](catalog/Unit-Classes.md) branches at every tier: Citizen opens into any base class, Myrmidon leads to Sword Master, Duelist *or* Griffon Knight, Soldier to Halberdier, Armored Knight *or* Pegasus Knight. Four promotions per unit means up to four irreversible decisions.
- **Skill Retention:** Abilities learned in a lower class are kept after promoting.
- **Level Retention:** Level stays the same across a promotion – levels are continuous 1–60, not per tier.
- **Stat Adjustments:** Class bonuses apply immediately.

**Consequence for the UI:** Because the choice cannot be undone, the branch options and their stat modifiers must be fully visible *before* the player commits. Design Pillar 5 demands the challenge be transparent – an irreversible decision made on hidden information would be punishment, not challenge.

**Consequence for balancing:** No branch may be a trap. A unit promoted down a weaker branch has no recovery path, so every option at a fork needs a niche where it is the right answer – see the "no strictly better options" principle in the [Balancing Guide](Balancing-Guide.md).

## ✅ Progression Checklist

Ensure each Part includes:

- [ ] 2-4 new playable units at the correct joining level for that Part
- [ ] Clear mechanical theme
- [ ] 1-2 major boss battles
- [ ] Story climax at Part finale
- [ ] Enemy levels 2 above the player average (Normal)

Additionally for Parts 05 and 06:

- [ ] Both strands use **identical** enemy level bands
- [ ] Both strands recruit the same number of units
